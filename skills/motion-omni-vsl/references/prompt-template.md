# Template de prompt

Bloco único de texto corrido, **em inglês**, nesta ordem. Faltando qualquer um, o Omni improvisa.

```
1. CRITICAL RULES   política de texto + likeness + obra original
2. STYLE            uma frase densa com o mundo visual
3. TECHNICAL        resolução, aspect, duração, e a regra de crop
4. TYPOGRAPHY       linha por linha, com a cor de cada uma
5. PALETTE          fundo, tipografia, acento e onde cada um pode aparecer
6. AUDIO            o que soa e o silêncio final
7. TIMELINE         os seis beats com timecode
8. CAMERA           como as coisas entram, saem e descansam
9. DO NOT           a lista de negações
```

**Blocos 2, 3, 5, 6, 8 e 9 são idênticos em todo o lote.** Só 1, 4 e 7 mudam.

---

## Blocos comuns (copiar sem alterar entre clipes do mesmo lote)

### TECHNICAL

> TECHNICAL: 1080x1920, 9:16 vertical portrait, 10 seconds, single continuous piece, no cuts to
> black, no letterboxing. The top 15 percent and the bottom 15 percent of the frame will later be
> cropped away, so every product, every graphic and every letter must sit comfortably inside the
> central 70 percent of the frame height. Nothing important touches the top or bottom edge.

### PALETTE

> PALETTE: Background pure black #0B0B0C. Typography and card line-art off-white #F6F3EC. The single
> accent is LeafTide green #3FB27A with real bloom — it appears ONLY as the edge light of a glass
> panel at the instant it lands, and as the glow on the emphasised line. Jars keep their own deep
> green #00562E body and #1B5E36 lid. No gold, no blue, no second accent.

### AUDIO

> AUDIO: No speech of any kind. A low cinematic bed with a soft sub pulse, one muted click when a
> panel lands, and complete silence for the final beat.

### CAMERA

> CAMERA: One continuous slow push-in for the whole piece, no cuts. Every element decelerates into
> place as if settling under its own weight — nothing moves at a constant speed. Elements that leave
> shrink and defocus rather than disappearing.

### DO NOT

> DO NOT: never render any word from these instructions as visible text — no technical terms, no
> "caption", no "safe zone", no "frame", no pixel sizes, no colour codes, no font names, no
> durations; no burned-in subtitles, no captions, no spoken words, no extra text of any kind beyond
> the exact phrase, no invented words, no gibberish lettering, no extra line of text, no headline
> above the phrase, no invented label copy, no fictional seals with writing, no gold accent, no lens
> flare streaks, no rapid strobing, no zoom punches, no watermark, no logo bug in the corner, no
> letterbox bars, no split screen, no people in frame.

## Arquétipo PURO — exemplo pronto

Frase de impacto, sem produto e sem gente. É o arquétipo mais confiável.

```
CRITICAL RULES — ONE TEXT EXCEPTION: The ONLY readable text allowed anywhere in this video is the
exact phrase "IT WILL NOT FIX ITSELF" — 5 words, nothing else. It must be spelled exactly like that,
letter for letter, and it appears as exactly 2 lines. Line 1 reads "IT WILL NOT". Line 2 reads
"FIX ITSELF". There is no additional line. There is no headline, no label, no sub-line, no kicker,
no extra word above, below or beside it. Never invent additional words to fill space. The phrase
appears EXACTLY ONCE in the frame at any moment — never duplicated on a second card, never repeated
elsewhere. Absolutely no other readable text, letters, numbers, subtitles, captions, watermarks or
UI copy may appear anywhere — every other panel and surface stays completely blank. NO SPEECH: there
is no person in frame, no dialogue, no voice-over, no narrator. This is an original motion graphics
composition.

STYLE: Premium direct-response motion graphics on a deep black void — a matte black stage with soft
volumetric haze and nothing else in frame but typography and one glass panel with thin luminous
green edges. Off-white line graphics only, subtle film grain, shallow depth of field, the restraint
of a high-end brand film. Vertical portrait composition: the panel and the stacked typography are
centred in the middle of the frame.

[TECHNICAL comum]

TYPOGRAPHY: Bold condensed geometric sans, all caps, off-white, tight tracking, generous line
spacing. Exactly 2 lines. Line 1: "IT WILL NOT" at base size in off-white. Line 2: "FIX ITSELF" one
size larger with a soft green glow. Nothing else is typeset anywhere in the frame.

[PALETTE comum]  [AUDIO comum]

TIMELINE: 0.0-1.5s the frame is empty black with slow drifting haze, camera pushing in very slowly,
no type at all. 1.5-3.5s a glass panel rises into the centre of the frame and its green edge lights
as it lands, still empty. 3.5-5.5s the words "IT WILL NOT" resolve inside the panel in off-white.
5.5-7.5s peak density — the words "FIX ITSELF" snap into place one size larger with a green bloom
and the whole block settles. 7.5-8.8s the glass panel shrinks, blurs and drifts away, leaving only
the phrase on black. 8.8-10.0s everything stops. The frame freezes, desaturates one step, a thin
green bar draws itself under the last line from left to right and halts. Silence.

[CAMERA comum]  [DO NOT comum]
```

## Arquétipo PRODUTO — o que muda

`CRITICAL RULES` ganha a trava do mockup:

> NO SPEECH: there is no person in frame, no dialogue, no voice-over, no narrator. The product jar
> must match the supplied reference image exactly — same deep green jar, same green screw lid, same
> label artwork and leaf motif. Do not invent a different label. Never place a badge, seal, sticker,
> ribbon or any writing on top of the jar itself. Never render the brand name as a separate card or
> label anywhere in the frame.

`STYLE` posiciona produto e texto:

> ... Vertical portrait composition: the jar sits in the upper middle of the frame and the typography
> stacks directly beneath it, both centred.

`TIMELINE` — mantenha **um** elemento gráfico por beat. Quanto mais coisa, mais superfície para o
modelo escrever besteira:

> 0.0-1.5s a single LeafTide jar alone in the black void in the upper middle of the frame, camera
> pushing in very slowly, absolutely no graphics and no text. 1.5-3.5s a plain glass panel with no
> writing rises directly beneath the jar and its green edge lights as it lands, the panel stays
> empty. 3.5-5.5s the words "100% MONEY BACK" resolve inside that panel in off-white — this is the
> only text that will ever appear. 5.5-7.5s peak density — the same words scale up one size and take
> on a green bloom, and a thin off-white ring with nothing written inside it draws once around the
> jar above. 7.5-8.8s the ring dissolves and the panel settles, the jar and the single line of text
> remain. 8.8-10.0s everything stops. ...
